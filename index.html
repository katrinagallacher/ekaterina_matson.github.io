Safe for whom? User awareness and its implications for LLM safety evaluation

Publication in progress

LLMs have an uncanny ability to infer information about the user they are interacting with, and to change their behavior depending on that user's persona. In the BlueDot Impact Technical AI Safety project, I reproduced and expanded Transluce's study on user awareness, answering two questions: how much this shift matters for AI safety evaluations, and what mechanism lies behind it.

A breakthrough feature: signs of tokenization awareness in LLMs

Published on Medium

I found a group of features in the early layers of Qwen3-4B and Gemma-2-2B that are involved in assembling concepts from tokens (detokenization). These features fire on specific types of compounds with an unstable tokenization pattern. The case study offers insight into token-merging quirks and showcases a specific type of meta-knowledge in LLMs: tokenization awareness.
