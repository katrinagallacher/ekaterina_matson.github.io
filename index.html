Current research

User awareness in frontier models

Active — BlueDot project course, extending Transluce's Aug 2026 work

Models infer who they are talking to and change their behaviour accordingly. Transluce documented the effect; I am asking two further questions: which inferred identities move a model toward or away from misaligned behaviour on a real safety eval, and what the mechanism looks like inside the network.

A pilot confirms the effect reproduces on the borderline-requests arm (OR-Bench hard-1k) in Qwen3-32B. Next is a less subtle safety benchmark across the full persona roster, compared using Transluce's statistical method, then the mechanistic substrate: layers, features, circuits.

Tokenization awareness in Qwen3-4B

Write-up published, code being packaged

A feature that tracks when a word has been split into pieces the model has to reassemble. I found it while looking at compound tokens, traced what it responds to, and wrote up what it implies for treating tokenization as a safety surface rather than a preprocessing detail.

Attractor states in paired-model dialogue

Pilot complete

The spiritual-convergence behaviour reported in Anthropic's Opus 4 system card, reproduced as an experiment rather than an anecdote. I built a pipeline that runs two local models against each other, then extracts and classifies where the conversation lands.

Qwen mirrored against Qwen does not drift toward contemplation — it drifts into tutorial mode. Different model, different fixed point, which is the interesting part.

Things I have built

A transformer small enough to read

Roughly two thousand parameters, every real component present, trained on detokenization so that the mechanism it learns is one you can follow end to end. Built for teaching, and for checking my own intuitions about what the big models are doing.

BPE merge tree viewer

A browser tool that shows how Gemma 2 and Qwen 3 arrive at their segmentations, side by side, so you can see where two tokenizers disagree about the same string.

Monthly tokenization digest

A short newsletter tracking new work on tokenization in language models — what came out, and what it changes.
